    Welcome to Battle for Rokugan

    A fast-paced board game of territory conquest, secret orders, bluffing, and honor in feudal Japan's Emerald Empire.

    🎯 Goal of the Game

    Accumulate the most Honor (★) by the end of Round 5.

    ⚔ How Honor is Earned

    • Provinces: Each controlled province grants 1–3 Honor (★ on map).
    • Territories: Controlling ALL provinces in a territory grants +3 Bonus Honor!
    • Secret Objectives: Each Daimyō is dealt a secret mission (e.g. control 3 coastlines) worth +4 to +5 Honor.

    The 3 Phases of Each Round

    The game plays over exactly 5 rounds. Each round has three distinct phases:

    1

    Upkeep Phase

    The First Player token rotates. Players draw tokens from their secret pool until they have 6 combat tokens in hand.

    2

    Placement Phase

    Taking turns clockwise, each player places 1 token face-down on the map. This repeats for 5 turns until each player has placed 5 tokens (keeping 1 secret in hand).

    3

    Resolution Phase

    All placed tokens are flipped face-up simultaneously! Raids burn lands, Diplomats secure peace, and armies clash for province control.

    Combat Tokens & Placement Rules

    Combat tokens have strict placement requirements:

    ARM +1..+4
    Army: Must be placed on a land border (originating from your territory, or defending your border), or inside your own province for defense. Cannot be placed on coastal borders.
    NAV +1..+3
    Navy: Must be placed on coastal borders (cyan dashed lines) or coastal provinces directly from the sea.
    SHI +1..+3
    Shinobi: Stealth operatives placed directly in the center of any province. Cannot be placed on borders!
    BLE +2
    Blessing: Must be placed inside a province you control (+3 strength for Phoenix).
    RAID 🔥
    Raid: Must be placed in the center of a province. Burns it to Scorched Earth (control lost, no tokens allowed).
    DIP 🕊
    Diplomat: Must be placed in a province you control. Brings permanent Peace (province can never be attacked again).
    BLUFF ★
    Bluff: Can be placed on any unoccupied border or any valid province. Returns to your hand upon reveal!

    How to Take Your Turn

    Playing on the board is intuitive with built-in legal move highlighting:

    Step 1

    Select a Token

    Click any token in your bottom dock. All legal deployment targets on the map immediately pulse in bright green/gold.

    Step 2

    Choose Target on Map

    Click a legal Province node or a Border hotspot. Illegal targets are automatically dimmed.

    Step 3

    Confirm Direction

    If placing on a border, a quick prompt asks which province you are attacking into.

    💡 Pro Tip: Defender always wins combat ties! Use Blessings and Province innate defense (⚔) to hold your lands against invaders.

    The 7 Great Clans & Special Powers

    Each Clan has an ancestral capital and a unique rule-bending ability:

    🦀 Crab Clan

    +1 Defense in all controlled provinces. Masters of defense holding the Kaiu Wall against the Shadowlands.

    🪶 Crane Clan

    Gain +2 Bonus Honor whenever placing a Diplomat. Masters of court, peace, and coastal diplomacy.

    🐉 Dragon Clan

    +1 Strength to all tokens placed inside your own provinces. Fierce defenders of high mountain sanctuaries.

    🦁 Lion Clan

    +1 Attack Strength with all Army tokens. Unstoppable military powerhouse of the central empire.

    🦅 Phoenix Clan

    Blessing tokens add +3 Strength instead of +2. Shugenja elementalists with spiritual warding.

    🦂 Scorpion Clan

    +1 Attack Strength with all Shinobi tokens. Masters of shadows, deception, and sudden assassinations.

    🐎 Unicorn Clan

    +1 Attack Strength in all non-capital provinces. Rapid cavalry raiders conquering the open borders.
